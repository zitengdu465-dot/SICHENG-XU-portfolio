# Structured Word Document Generation

[Portfolio home](README.md) · [All projects](CATALOG.md)

Node.js scripts that construct repeatable Word documents from explicit document and formatting structures.

**Technologies:** Node.js · JavaScript · docx

**Status:** Implemented document-specific generators.

## Problem

Repeatedly formatting structured documents by hand makes numbering, tables, paragraph spacing and typographic consistency harder to maintain.

## My work

- Developed three generators for an agreement, articles of association and an associated checklist.
- Defined reusable paragraph, clause, heading and text helpers using the docx library.
- Controlled page structure, fonts, tables, headers/footers and output destinations in code.

## Evidence and results

Three source scripts and their dependency manifest are present. The scripts construct Word document objects and accept an output path.

## Scope and limitations

These are generators for specific documents, with document text embedded in the scripts. They are not a general legal drafting system, contract-analysis service or legal-advice product. Business text and generated legal documents are not included here.
