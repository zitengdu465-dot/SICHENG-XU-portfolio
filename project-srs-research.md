# Stimulated Raman Scattering: Undergraduate Research

[Portfolio home](README.md) · [All projects](CATALOG.md)

A thesis investigating stimulated Raman scattering gain for a super-Gaussian–Maxwellian electron distribution.

**Technologies:** Kinetic theory · Numerical analysis · Scientific communication

**Status:** Thesis available locally; a reproducible code release has not been assembled.

## Problem

Laser–plasma interactions depend on the electron distribution. This research examines how a combined super-Gaussian–Maxwellian distribution affects stimulated Raman scattering gain.

## My work

- Studied a one-dimensional theoretical model based on the gain factor.
- Analysed the dependence of gain and scattered-light wavelength on distribution and plasma parameters.
- Presented numerical figures and discussed the implications in an undergraduate thesis.

## Evidence and results

The thesis includes the theoretical derivation, a numerical-analysis chapter and result figures. Its title and abstract were inspected during portfolio preparation.
